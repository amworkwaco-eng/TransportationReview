Access Determination

Step 1 establishes the access management standards from CDC 501-8.5 for each frontage road. Step 2 evaluates existing driveways against those standards. Step 3 captures proposed driveways. Step 4 evaluates legally existing non-conforming accesses under CDC 440-10 and identifies required documents.

Constitutional Analysis — Nollan / Dolan

For each exaction triggered by this case, document: (1) the legitimate government interest, (2) the essential nexus to the development's impact, and (3) the rough proportionality.
